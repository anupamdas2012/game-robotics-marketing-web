A day with Lex

He's not a gadget. He's a character.

Lex Jr. doesn't just respond to commands — he notices, remembers, and reacts. Little moments that make him feel alive.

👋

He knows your face.

Walk in the door and Lex looks up, tracks you, and lights up with a specific greeting — because he actually recognizes you, not just "a human."

🎭

He mirrors your mood.

Smile and he brightens. Look down and he gets quieter, softer. Expression isn't a canned animation — it's a live reaction to you.

🧠

He remembers what matters.

Names, routines, the songs you liked, the plant he watched you water. Memory that persists, so tomorrow's Lex knows more than today's.

🐾

He wanders. He's curious.

Lex explores on his own. He'll come find you, poke at something new, and get excited when the world changes. Presence, not just presence-detection.

Under the hood

Everything you'd expect. Nothing you'd have to build.

Lex Jr. ships with a full stack of perception, expression, motion and on-device intelligence. It's not magic. It's just usually really hard.

👀

Perception

RGB + depth cameras, face + object recognition, spatial audio, on-device.

💛

Personality

Live emotional state, expressive eyes and voice, reactions tuned to context.

🧠

On-device AI

Local language + vision models. Private by default, memory that persists.

🤖

Motion

Smooth, coordinated servos. Wanders, tracks, orients — never robotic-jerky.

🔌

Hackable

USB-C, GPIO, an open SDK. Add sensors, swap behaviors, remap everything.

📡

Always improving

Over-the-air updates. New behaviors, new tricks, new personality drops.
